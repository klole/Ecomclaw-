# OpenClaw Agent Roles

## 1. Ad Creative Intelligence Agent

**What it does:**
- Monitors competitor ad libraries (Facebook Ad Library, TikTok Creative Center)
- Tracks viral content in their niche (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts)
- Analyzes what's working (hooks, formats, music, CTAs)
- Generates creative briefs for their team/agency with specific examples
- Tracks performance of their own ads and suggests iterations
- Auto-generates ad copy variations based on winning patterns

**Value Prop:** "Never run out of ad ideas. Get 10 winning ad concepts every week based on what's actually working in your niche."

**Price:** $297 setup + $197/mo

## 2. PPC Optimization & A/B Testing Agent

**What it does:**
- Monitors ROAS in real-time across Facebook, Google, TikTok
- Automatically pauses underperforming ads when ROAS drops below threshold
- Scales winning ads by increasing budget automatically
- Runs continuous A/B tests on ad copy, creatives, audiences
- Sends daily spend reports with insights ("You spent $500 on this campaign with 1.2 ROAS - recommend pausing")
- Tracks attribution windows and adjusts bidding strategies
- Monitors CPM/CPC trends and alerts when costs spike

**Value Prop:** "Stop wasting money on bad ads. Automatically pause losers and scale winners 24/7."

**Price:** $497 setup + $297/mo
